Manual: how a protein becomes music

Time scales

  • Song: key from mean hydropathy, major or minor from net charge (auto styles), seed from the sequence.
  • Section (64 residues): majority secondary structure, with hysteresis so it does not flicker. Sets energy, chord pool, instruments and the visual.
  • Block (16 residues): one chord; smoothed hydropathy shapes melody contour, filters and color; charge density sets rhythm density.
  • Step (1 residue): with BIO coupling, a residue can pick the next melodic interval, accents and ghost notes.

Controls

  • TEMPO: live; double-click a knob to reset it.
  • SMOOTH: window of the hydropathy average (1 = raw).
  • BIO: 0% = free composition steered by slow features, 100% = residues pick notes.
  • VAR: a new composition for the same protein.
  • RAW 1:1: the naive mapping (each residue sets pitch and loudness) for comparison.
